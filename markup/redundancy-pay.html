      How was this calculated?

      Enter your details to see how the figures are worked out.

      Statutory redundancy pay is half a week’s pay for each full year you were under 22, one week’s pay for each full year you were 22 to 40, and one and a half weeks’ pay for each full year you were 41 or older. Years are counted back from the date your job ends, up to 20 years. Weekly pay is your pay before tax, capped at £751 a week (£783 in Northern Ireland) for redundancies from 6 April 2026, so the most anyone can get is £22,530 (£23,490 in Northern Ireland). Ages are in whole years, so the result can differ slightly from your employer’s if you have a birthday close to a year of service ending.
