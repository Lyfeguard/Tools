09 / UNDERSTANDING THE FIGURES

How was this calculated?

The calculation uses the tax rules for the selected period, but the correct IHT treatment of an actual estate may depend on valuations, gifts, residence eligibility and relief claims.

Add values above to see which rules affect the result.
Standard and inherited allowances+
The individual nil-rate band is £325,000. A surviving spouse or civil partner may claim the unused proportion of their late partner's band, potentially raising the combined standard allowance to £650,000. Chargeable gifts in the previous seven years may reduce the band available against the estate.
The extra home allowance and £2m taper+
Where a qualifying home or share passes to direct descendants, the residence nil-rate band may add up to £175,000 per person, plus an eligible transferred amount. It cannot exceed the home equity closely inherited and reduces by £1 for every £2 by which the net estate exceeds £2m. The taper test uses the estate after debts, before spouse/charity exemptions and business reliefs.
Why charitable gifts can reduce the rate+
Qualifying charitable bequests are exempt. A reduced 36% rate may apply if the charitable gift is at least 10% of a defined baseline amount. The headline calculation here uses 40%; it does not automatically apply the reduced rate. Where the simple 10% test is met, a possible 36% illustration appears in the explanation, but it is not a confirmed liability. Mixed estates, pensions, trusts and gifts free of tax can require a different HMRC calculation.
Gifts, business relief and pensions+
This calculator uses declared non-exempt lifetime gifts and confirmed business/agricultural relief but does not calculate separate inheritance tax on lifetime gifts or all relief eligibility conditions. From 6 April 2027 most unused pension funds and death benefits fall within IHT, with exceptions. Enter amounts already within IHT and additional post-2027 pension values separately.
Scope: simplified UK estate estimate. Trusts, non-UK assets and long-term residence, downsizing relief, lifetime gift tax and taper, seven-year gift ordering, multiple estate components, grossing-up, jointly owned property, tax-free legacies and some pensions require specialist treatment.
