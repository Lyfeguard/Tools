Worked example: a £280,000 repayment mortgage

Buying a £350,000 home with a £70,000 deposit means borrowing £280,000 (80% loan-to-value). At 4.5% over 25 years on a repayment mortgage, the monthly payment is £1,556.33.

Over the full term that's £186,899.28 in interest and £466,899.28 repaid in total. At 3.5% the payment would be £1,401.75 a month; at 5.5%, £1,719.44.
