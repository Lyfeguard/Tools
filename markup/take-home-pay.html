        How was this calculated?

        Enter your pay to see how the figures are worked out.

        Uses 2026/27 rates: a Personal Allowance of £12,570, which shrinks by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000; income tax at England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates (20%, 40%, 45%) or Scottish rates (19% to 48%); employee National Insurance (Class 1, category A); and student loan thresholds for the year. Pay is spread evenly over the year, so a month is a twelfth and a week is a fifty-second. Pension taken before tax gets tax relief straight away; with relief at source your provider adds 20% and you claim any higher-rate relief yourself; salary sacrifice also saves National Insurance. Employer pension contributions, taxable benefits and tax code adjustments are not included.
