      Uses the main residential rates for Wales from 10 October 2022: 0% up to £225,000, 6% from £225,001 to £400,000, 7.5% to £750,000, 10% to £1.5 million and 12% above. An additional property costing £40,000 or more pays the higher residential rates from 11 December 2024: 5% up to £180,000, 8.5% to £250,000, 10% to £400,000, 12.5% to £750,000, 15% to £1.5 million and 17% above. The total is rounded down to the pound. England and Northern Ireland have Stamp Duty Land Tax and Scotland has Land and Buildings Transaction Tax instead.
