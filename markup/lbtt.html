      How was this calculated?

      Enter the price to see how the figures are worked out.

      Uses the residential rates for Scotland: 0% up to £145,000, 2% from £145,001 to £250,000, 5% to £325,000, 10% to £750,000 and 12% above. First-time buyer relief raises the 0% band to £175,000, saving up to £600, whatever the price. An additional property costing £40,000 or more also pays the Additional Dwelling Supplement of 8% of the whole price. The total is rounded down to the pound. England and Northern Ireland have Stamp Duty Land Tax and Wales has Land Transaction Tax instead.
