      How was this calculated?

      Enter the price to see how the figures are worked out.

      Uses the residential rates for England and Northern Ireland from 1 April 2025: 0% up to £125,000, 2% from £125,001 to £250,000, 5% to £925,000, 10% to £1.5 million and 12% above. First-time buyers pay nothing up to £300,000 and 5% from £300,001 to £500,000; above £500,000 the standard rates apply. An additional property costing £40,000 or more pays 5% more on every band, and non-UK residents pay 2% more. The total is rounded down to the pound. Scotland has Land and Buildings Transaction Tax and Wales has Land Transaction Tax instead.
