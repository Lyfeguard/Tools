    How was this calculated?

    Choose your council to see how the figures are worked out.

    Councils in England set a Band D amount each year, and every other band pays a fixed share of it, in ninths: Band A 6/9, B 7/9, C 8/9, D 9/9, E 11/9, F 13/9, G 15/9 and H 18/9 (Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 5). We use each council’s average Band D for 2026/27 from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, which includes the county council, police, fire, mayor and the average town or parish council charge. The single person discount takes 25% off. Monthly figures divide the year’s bill into 10 or 12 equal payments; councils round the first payment slightly differently.
