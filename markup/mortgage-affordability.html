      How was this calculated?

      Enter your details to see how the figures are worked out.

      Lenders mostly lend a multiple of your income: 4 to 4.5 times is typical, many go up to 6 times for higher incomes or bigger deposits, and a few specialist lenders go up to 7 times. We count basic pay in full and bonus, overtime and commission at half, then take off a year of your commitments: loan and car finance repayments, credit cards (3% of the balance a month, or your minimum payments) and other committed spending. The term is limited so the mortgage ends by age 75. Take-home pay uses 2026/27 income tax and National Insurance, after the pension and other deductions you enter; for two of you, the pay is split equally. Debt-to-income is your debt repayments, including the mortgage, as a share of your pay before tax. These are typical ranges, not any lender’s criteria.
