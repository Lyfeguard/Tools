      How was this calculated?

      Enter your details to see how the figures are worked out.

      Lenders mostly lend a multiple of your income: 4 to 4.5 times is typical, many go up to 6 times for higher incomes or bigger deposits, and a few specialist lenders go up to 7 times. We count basic pay in full and bonus, overtime and commission at half, then take off a year of your commitments: loan and car finance repayments, credit cards (3% of the balance a month, or your minimum payments) and other committed spending. Take-home pay uses 2026/27 income tax and National Insurance for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, before pension or student loan deductions. Debt-to-income is your debt repayments, including this mortgage, as a share of your pay before tax. These are typical ranges, not any lender’s criteria.
