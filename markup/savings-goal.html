How was this calculated?

We start with your existing savings, add interest and then add your saving at the end of each month. Interest stays in the account and can earn further interest.

Monthly saving needed

Without interest, we subtract your starting savings from your target and divide the remaining amount by the number of months. With interest, we convert your AER to an equivalent monthly rate first.

Monthly rate r = (1 + AER / 100)^(1 / 12) − 1
Monthly saving = max(0, (T − P × (1 + r)^n) / (((1 + r)^n − 1) / r))

T is your target, P is your starting savings and n is the number of months. At 0% interest, monthly saving = max(0, (T − P) / n). We round the required monthly saving up to a sufficient penny.

Time to your goal

We project one month at a time until the balance reaches your target. The result is the first whole month in which you could reach it. If your starting savings already cover the target, no further saving is needed.

Assumptions and limits

The rate stays constant, deposits are equal and interest remains in the account. Every deposit, including the final one, is the full monthly amount, so the final balance may exceed your target. Your bank’s daily calculations and payment dates can produce a different result.

The table shows cumulative figures. Total paid in includes your starting savings and subsequent deposits. Interest is the estimated total earned by that point. Calculations retain full precision; displayed money is rounded to pennies.

The calculator excludes withdrawals, tax, fees, inflation, changing rates, account limits, introductory rates and bonuses, including Lifetime ISA bonuses. It models cash savings and does not model investment risk.

You can model up to 50 years, targets and starting savings up to £10 million, and AERs from 0% to 25%. In time-to-goal mode, monthly saving can be up to £1 million. These are calculation limits, not suggested amounts or rates.

For more detail, see HSBC’s explanation of AER and GOV.UK’s guidance on savings interest and tax.

Worked example: saving £10,000 in 2½ years

To reach £10,000 in 2 years and 6 months, starting with £1,000 in an account paying 4% AER, you'd need to save £282.73 a month.

You'd pay in £8,481.90 on top of your starting £1,000, and earn £518.23 in interest.
